The selected modelling architecture is direct EO inside the ERV

The EO membrane shares the counter-current air passages of the ERV. Post-ERV O30 work remains a coupon-test boundary and historical comparator, not the selected system architecture.

Voltage is prescribed in the steady axial model; current is solved

For each axial position, the local humidity and temperature determine surface activities and material properties. The voltage balance is then solved for the local current density.

Vapp = j[ASRmem(aw) + ASRseries] + twRT/F · ln(ar,s/as,s) + Vfixed

Vapp

Local response

j(x)

Current density changes with hydration, films and surface activities.

Water flux

Nw(x)

EO drag and passive back-diffusion are combined locally.

Nw = twj/F − D(aw)S(aw) · daw/dx
The symbols “supply side” and “receiver side” do not mean two independent currents. At a given axial station, the same through-plane current magnitude crosses the membrane; the sign changes only if each surface uses its own outward-normal convention.

Humidity and current are recalculated along the core

The counter-current air states evolve along the ERV length. Every segment receives updated bulk states, solves the two gas-film boundaries, solves the membrane, and advances both air-stream water balances.

Purely blocking electrodes provide a finite startup displacement

Charge accumulates on the electrode pair until the terminal-voltage clamp limits the current. With ideal blocking electrodes, current and incremental EO transport decay toward zero.

Complete-cycle invariant

For an ideal symmetric blocking pair with constant drag, a charge-balanced cycle gives zero net EO water transport.

Model limitation

The replay freezes the ERV bulk states and uses an unmeasured capacitance sensitivity. It is a duration diagnostic, not a dynamic ERV prediction.

The terminal-voltage limit is not an individual-electrode electrochemical window. Individual electrode potentials and reaction limits are not resolved by this reduced model.

Synthetic parameter combinations are design coordinates—not materials

The requested tw = 50 and Dref = 10−10 m²/s combination was run as a synthetic sensitivity. It helps map the model, but it does not identify a realistic membrane.
