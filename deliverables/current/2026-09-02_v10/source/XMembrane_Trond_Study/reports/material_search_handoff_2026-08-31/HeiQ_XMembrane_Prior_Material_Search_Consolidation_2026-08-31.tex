\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

The local record contains extensive previous material work, but it is spread
across literature registers, ion screens, Nafion references, inverse-design
studies, downloaded reports and thousands of synthetic model coordinates. The
work did not identify a commercially validated ``optimal membrane.'' Its most
defensible real-material result is Selemion CMVN: in liquid single-salt tests
at $295\pm1$~K, reported water drag was
$1.1\pm0.8$, $9.2\pm0.8$, $4.9\pm0.2$ and $3.7\pm0.4$ mol H$_2$O per mol
charge for H$^+$, Li$^+$, Na$^+$ and K$^+$ forms. This makes Li$^+$ the best
same-platform counter-ion hypothesis and Selemion the strongest local
mechanism coupon, not a demonstrated system-performance candidate. CR61 AZL
386, MF-4SC, Nafion 117/120 and BPSH contribute useful property fragments;
microporous polyamine, plaster--silica, perforated ion-exchange and graded or
adsorbing composites remain literature/architecture leads.

The historical V12--V15 sequence supports the Li$^+$ route but also shows why
it cannot be promoted directly to procurement ranking. V12's cross-study
prior gave Li$^+$ a median conditional flux of 0.442~kg,m$^{-2}$,h$^{-1}$
and SEC of 0.125~kWh/kg at 0.6~V. V13 identified wet vapour conductivity as
the dominant information gap, followed by drag and diffusivity. V14's
supported 25-$\mu$m checkpoint remained conditional, and V15 reduced its bulk
performance once two vapour interfaces were introduced; favourable-gradient
flux was about 2.68 times the opposing-gradient result. The current direct-EO
and active-ERV studies likewise define sampled property regions, not material
recipes.

The earlier transport numbers above 100 are fully retained: they originated
in hypothetical V4 and later inverse-design sweeps extending to
$t_w=150$, while the current Terminal-1 vector has $t_w=100.0608$.
Neither is a measured material. No named local candidate has a complete,
same-sample set of sorption, chemical water mobility, EO drag, wet
through-plane conductivity, compressed-stack ASR, swelling/thickness,
two-sided flux, interface compatibility and provenance. The immediate step is
therefore an availability-and-coupon shortlist built from the existing leads,
followed by one controlled protocol on the same physical samples. Candidate
ranking and net energy claims remain open until that evidence exists.

The governing V8 decision sequence makes post-ERV O30 the first
system-relevant coupon target. It removes 1,228.27~kg/h on the declared
boundary and has sampled solutions at 1.00--1.25~V for every one-factor
$D=10^{-14}$--$10^{-9}$~m$^2$/s coordinate, although the conditional power
window deteriorates from $+162.416$ to $-181.413$~kW. Direct EO inside the ERV
is a separate secondary architecture: 178/320 rows close transport, 50/320
pass the engineering screen and 0/320 pass the full coupled gate. Neither
screen identifies a physical membrane or a net system saving.

\begin{mainbox}
\textbf{Decision.} Do not perform another broad hypothetical material sweep.
Begin supplier/availability consolidation and coupon qualification using the
existing search results. Start with the Li$^+$-form CEM/ionomer route and a
Selemion CMVN evidence anchor, retain non-PFSA and microporous/composite routes,
and use Nafion only as a reference control.
\end{mainbox}

The active-ERV Terminal-1 coordinate is
\begin{equation}
  t_w=100.0608,\quad D_{\rm ref}=1.598\times10^{-14}\ \mathrm{m^2/s},
  \quad \sigma_{\rm ref}=23.1719\ \mathrm{S/m},\quad L=36.9137\ \mu\mathrm{m}.
\end{equation}
It is a synthetic inverse target, not ``the optimal membrane.'' In addition,
the physically relevant passive term is not scalar $D$ by itself but the
chemical-permeance bundle
\begin{equation}
  K_{a,w}\propto \frac{D(a_w,T)}{L}\frac{\partial C_w}{\partial a_w},
\end{equation}
together with drag, conductivity/ASR and source/receiver films. A favourable
ERV gradient can assist passive transport before the pinch, so blindly
minimising permeance may also remove useful transfer.

\begin{stopbox}
Do not build a fictitious candidate by taking drag from Selemion, conductivity
from MF-4SC, diffusivity from Nafion 120 and thickness from an inverse-design
row. That combination has never existed on one membrane. The previous studies
used such combinations deliberately as hypothesis space; the next phase must
test whether the required properties coexist.
\end{stopbox}

\subsection{Recommended first coupon matrix}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Confirm whether Selemion CMVN of known construction and lot is
    obtainable. Prepare Li$^+$, Na$^+$, K$^+$ and H$^+$ forms from the same lot
    where chemically defensible. Record sheet dimensions, support/open area,
    minimum order and the number of usable 80-by-80-mm coupons. This is the
    cleanest local counter-ion test, not a performance ranking.
  \item Run NR211 and NR212 in the identical fixture as thin/thickness PFSA
    controls. They validate the method; they do not define the target.
  \item Add one obtainable non-PFSA sulfonated aromatic/BPSH-like membrane and
    one microporous/adsorbing architecture coupon when identity and construction
    are traceable.
  \item Use split common inlet, foil blank, zero-current positive/negative
    gradients and ABBA/BAAB polarity blocks. Close source loss against receiver
    gain; repeat masks, gap and flow; keep $\Delta p$ within $\pm5$~Pa and run
    separate 0, $\pm20$ and $\pm50$~Pa pressure challenges.
  \item Use the same physical coupon for sorption, mobility, drag, conductivity
    and stack ASR wherever the method permits. Freeze replicate count only
    after the pilot and conditioning-yield check.
