# Direct-ERV gradient closure v1

**Phase decision:** the broad steady-state model study can stop; start joint-property material and coupon work.

## Closed findings

- O30 retains a favourable bulk activity margin, but the selected local flux reverses the membrane-surface margin to -0.00797.
- A common film-capacity improvement or diagnostic area increase of 1.137x is sufficient to restore a non-negative surface direction at fixed duty; 1.733x is required for the former +0.02 robustness margin.
- The full-duty selected coordinate has zero bulk activity margin at the counter-current pinch. No finite film/area multiplier can create a positive surface margin at finite local flux; increasing area only approaches zero.
- The matched +J/0/-J replay gives an O30 incremental even component of 0.00151783 kg/h (0.348 ppm of the +J rate).
- The full-duty incremental even component is 0 kg/h. Within the present constant-drag steady model, charge-balanced symmetric current therefore adds no useful mean EO transport beyond N(0).

## Claim boundary

The signed replay is quasi-static and current-symmetric. It does not predict frequency response, hysteresis, induced-charge EO, electrode rectification or a spatially graded membrane. A symmetric voltage waveform also does not guarantee zero mean charge when a chemical-potential offset exists.

Area is a diagnostic throughout this closure study, not an acceptance gate. Positive passive surface direction is a regime diagnostic, not a thermodynamic feasibility gate for active EO.
