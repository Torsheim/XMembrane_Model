# Claim boundary

The model phase is complete. The current model supports physics-based
numerical feasibility, inverse-design, sensitivity studies and experiment
design. It does not constitute technology validation.

The current model does not establish that:

- the selected independent material-property vector exists experimentally;
- the required receiver resource is practically available;
- the complete membrane module has been mechanically validated;
- electrode and current-collector architecture has been validated;
- long-duration material stability is known;
- system economics have been experimentally demonstrated.

The steady voltage-controlled transport results additionally require a
mechanism that can sustain ionic current. The reduced-order transient replay
shows that two purely blocking capacitive electrodes provide only a finite
startup displacement while charging. Symmetric charge-balanced cycling gives
zero net electro-osmotic water transport over a complete cycle in the current
constant-drag model. A long-duration EO claim therefore requires measured
evidence for a Faradaic, pseudocapacitive, rectifying, flow-switching or other
regenerable cycle mechanism. Until then, steady-current results are explicitly
counterfactual operating coordinates rather than demonstrated duty points.

The frozen ERV transient is interpreted first at each stored case's original
terminal-voltage limit. A separate replay that commands the stored current up
to 1.8 V is an optimistic capacity upper-bound sensitivity, not a same-voltage
replay. Neither terminal-voltage limit constrains or resolves the individual
electrode potentials or an electrode-pair electrochemical stability window;
those require measurement and a separate gate.

The selected modelling architecture for final decisions is EO integrated in
the ERV membrane/core. Post-ERV O30 remains a controlled coupon-test boundary
and historical comparison, not a second selected product architecture.

The combined `t_w=50`, `D=1e-10 m2/s` replay is a synthetic post-release
sensitivity point. It is not a named or validated material, has no
full-coupled system pass and does not alter the final claim boundary.

Results should therefore be described as model predictions, inverse-design
targets, conditional energy results, transient diagnostics or engineering
screening values unless experimental validation is explicitly available. No
current result authorises a measured membrane, module, net-system energy,
commercial-performance or continuous-operation claim.
